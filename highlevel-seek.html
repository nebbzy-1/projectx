Product map · early concept

One place to find and track the jobs worth applying for.

A simple pipeline takes searches from multiple job sources, applies the user’s criteria, removes duplicates, and presents a clean list with application tracking.

Tap a focus button to emphasize that stage.

How the app works

Check listings searches Indeed and Glassdoor with JobSpy, imports labelled SEEK alerts from Gmail, and then checks saved listing links for expiry.
